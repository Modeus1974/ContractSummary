What this program does not do

Classical NLP techniques such as tokenisation, stemming, or named-entity recognition, run as separate pipeline stages. A single large language model call replaces all of them. The trade-off: fast to build, and accurate most of the time, but only as verifiable as step 4 makes it. Nothing here proves the model's understanding was correct beyond the handful of concrete figures step 4 can actually check.
