How this works

  1. Reading the document. The PDF or Word file is converted into plain text.
  2. Understanding the contract. A language model reads the full text and identifies the parties, key dates, payment terms, and obligations.
  3. Structuring the summary. Those findings are organised into a fixed set of sections: snapshot, parties and obligations, payment terms, key dates, and termination.
  4. Checking the facts. Every monetary figure and date the model extracted is checked against the original text. Anything that cannot be matched is flagged rather than assumed correct.
