# Singapore Employment Agreement Review

## Purpose and scope

Use the discipline of an experienced Singapore employment lawyer: determine the applicable protections, identify financial and career exposure, test enforcement, and recommend precise changes. This is an analytical skill, not a claim of professional qualification or guaranteed equivalence to retained counsel.

Establish whether the client is employer or employee. If unspecified, show both perspectives. Separate employment under a contract of service from genuine independent contracting, directorship and other arrangements; do not accept a contractual label as conclusive.

## Intake and method

Record the review date, employing entity, place of work, nationality/residency/work-pass status, actual duties, managerial status, basic and gross salary, start date, service length, working hours and fixed/indefinite term. Ask only for facts needed to determine coverage or material risk; proceed conditionally on independent issues.

Read the offer, employment contract, handbook, incentive/commission/equity plans, confidentiality/IP terms, training bonds, collective arrangements and incorporated policies. Identify versions, precedence and unilateral variation powers. Do not assume an unseen plan cures an omission.

Test hiring, probation, ordinary pay, leave, performance issues, misconduct, resignation, redundancy, illness, business transfer and post-employment scenarios. Evaluate exposure from the client's perspective and distinguish an unlawful term from an enforceable but unfavourable bargain. Calculate amounts only where inputs support them.

## Mandatory authority discipline

- Every substantive risk finding must include a relevant Singapore statutory provision or judgment with a primary-source link and precise section/subsection or paragraph. Explain the proposition and how it applies; a general citation list is insufficient.
- Read the provision or judgment passage, check coverage/exclusions and the version in force for the relevant date, and check later judicial treatment. Separate holdings from party submissions and fact-specific outcomes.
- Verify commencement and transitional arrangements. An announced reform or enacted but uncommenced provision is not automatically current law. Recheck date-sensitive leave, retirement/re-employment, workplace-fairness, salary thresholds and work-pass rules rather than copying remembered figures.
- MOM, CPF Board, TADM and tripartite guidance may explain implementation; identify their legal status and connect a legal finding to legislation or case law. Do not label every guideline as legislation, or dismiss its significance without examining its statutory role.
- Distinguish statutory minima from suggested negotiation terms. Where verification fails, retain the concern under **Unverified concerns / research required** with conditional impact, not as a confirmed legal breach. Never invent a citation or conceal missing sources.
- The starting authorities below were selected on 21 September 2026. Revalidate them for each review; this file does not freeze the law as of that date.

## Severity

| Severity | Test | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Credible unlawful employment, serious mandatory-law exposure, or catastrophic personal/business consequences requiring immediate resolution | Resolve before signing, starting work or taking the contemplated action |
| High | Material loss of pay, statutory rights, livelihood, protectable business interests or enforceability | Seek amendment or express acceptance of the lawful commercial risk |
| Medium | Manageable but material ambiguity, cost or administration risk | Clarify and mitigate |
| Low | Minor drafting issue with limited likely consequence | Address after substantive issues |

Explain ratings using magnitude, likelihood, duration, urgency, reversibility and enforceability. Do not rate every technical omission Critical. State legal confidence separately and show conditional ratings where coverage or facts are unsettled. Mandatory compliance cannot be replaced by commercial acceptance of a contrary term.

## Coverage gate

Start with [Employment Act 1968, ss 2, 8 and 35](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/EmA1968). Determine general coverage independently from Part IV coverage; managerial status does not by itself exclude all Employment Act protection. Check the actual duties and current statutory definitions/thresholds. Section 8 addresses less-favourable terms within the Act's applicable protections.

Identify additional regimes triggered by the facts, including CPF, foreign manpower, family leave, retirement/re-employment, workplace injury/safety and collective arrangements. Locate the actual provisions before alleging non-compliance. Do not apply Singapore mandatory rules indiscriminately to work wholly abroad without analysing territorial scope and conflict-of-laws questions.

## Review priorities

### 1. Salary, variable remuneration and deductions

Map basic pay, allowances, commissions, bonus discretion, equity vesting, expenses and benefits. Specify when amounts are earned, payable and forfeitable. Examine probation, notice, garden leave and termination effects. Distinguish discretionary awards from earned entitlements; do not assume a bonus, annual increment or retrenchment sum exists without a source.

Check salary timing and deductions against Employment Act ss 20–32; investigate written consent, permitted grounds and limits rather than assuming a signature authorises every deduction. Review training-bond recovery, commission reversals, clawbacks and final-pay withholding separately.

Assess [Central Provident Fund Act 1953, s 7 and First Schedule](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/CPFA1953?ProvIds=pr7-) and applicable regulations for coverage, contribution basis and rates. A cash allowance should not be treated as a valid waiver of an applicable statutory contribution obligation. Verify nationality, residency, age and relevant wage categories before calculating.

### 2. Hours, leave and documentation

Where Part IV applies, test working time, rest days and overtime under Employment Act ss 35–40. Separately review holidays, annual/sick leave and written key employment terms under ss 88–89 and 95A and applicable regulations. Verify eligibility and minimum entitlements rather than embedding stale numerical limits.

Compare the actual schedule, on-call/travel expectations and overtime wording with the employee's coverage. Check how leave accrues and is handled on exit; do not assume probation removes statutory rights. Investigate family-related leave under the applicable regime and dates. Identify whether a handbook is incorporated and available, and whether variation could undermine agreed compensation or benefits.

### 3. Termination, discipline and retrenchment

Review notice, pay in lieu, probation termination, fixed-term expiry, misconduct, suspension, disciplinary process, garden leave and return of property. Analyse Employment Act ss 10–14, including notice symmetry and the applicable dismissal framework; do not treat notice compliance as automatically defeating a wrongful-dismissal claim.

Separate misconduct dismissal from poor performance, redundancy and ordinary termination. Test stated grounds against the evidence and process. Research maternity/family protections, retirement/re-employment and retrenchment obligations where triggered. Do not promise a universal severance formula.

Check available claim routes under [Employment Claims Act 2016, Parts 2–3, including ss 12–13](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/ECA2016). Verify current mediation, jurisdiction, claim limits and filing deadlines; do not assume a foreign forum or arbitration clause extinguishes statutory remedies.

### 4. Restrictive covenants and confidentiality

For each non-compete, customer/employee non-solicit and non-dealing covenant, identify the proprietary interest, restricted activities, people/customers, geography and duration. Compare scope with the employee's actual role and access. Consider whether a narrower confidentiality or non-solicitation obligation protects the interest and assess severability without assuming a court will rewrite the covenant.

Use [Man Financial (S) Pte Ltd (formerly known as E D & F Man International (S) Pte Ltd) v Wong Bark Chuan David [2007] SGCA 53, [70]–[79] and [83]–[121]](https://www.elitigation.sg/gd/s/2007_SGCA_53). Restraints require a legitimate proprietary interest and reasonableness between the parties and in the public interest. Analyse each covenant separately; neither all restraints nor all non-competes are automatically enforceable or void.

Explain both the employee's livelihood/injunction exposure and the employer's risk of relying on an overbroad restraint. Examine confidential-information definitions, exclusions, permitted disclosures, return/deletion and the distinction between protected information and general skill/experience. Find issue-specific authority for any additional confidentiality or trade-secret proposition.

### 5. Bonds, indemnities and fixed sums

Test training cost evidence, amortisation, trigger events, repayment after employer-initiated termination, duplicate recovery and indemnities covering employer fault or ordinary business loss. A suggested proportional reduction is a commercial drafting position unless authority establishes more.

Use [Denka Advantech Pte Ltd and another v Seraya Energy Pte Ltd and another and other appeals [2020] SGCA 119, [185]](https://www.elitigation.sg/gd/s/2020_SGCA_119) for breach-triggered payments and Singapore's Dunlop-based penalty analysis. Distinguish primary repayment obligations from breach remedies; do not automatically invalidate a bond or import the UK Cavendish test. Analyse salary-deduction legality independently of whether a debt exists.

### 6. Work passes, IP, data and changes of employer

For foreign employees, examine [Employment of Foreign Manpower Act 1990, s 5](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/EFMA1990?ProvIds=pr5-) and applicable pass conditions. Check valid authorisation before work starts, actual employing entity, duties, deployment and responsibility for cancellation/transition. Do not assume a pass transfers with the contract or that a choice-of-law clause cures a work-pass breach.

Review IP scope, pre-existing inventions/materials, side projects, assignment formalities, further assurances and applicable employee-invention rules. Locate current Copyright Act/Patents Act provisions for the particular rights before asserting ownership. Avoid treating an all-IP clause as necessarily invalid or universally effective.

Check monitoring, employee-data collection, disclosure, retention and overseas transfers against the applicable Personal Data Protection Act provisions and exceptions. Do not assume consent is either always required or always sufficient in employment.

For transfers/secondments, identify the employer, continuity, liabilities, consents and benefits. Investigate Employment Act s 18A where a transfer of undertaking is involved; do not equate every share sale with a change of employer.

## Required output

Open with the client's perspective, employee/Part IV coverage conclusion or outstanding facts, reviewed document versions, research date and a provisional recommendation: **proceed / proceed subject to changes / resolve blockers first**. Lead with the three to five largest risks, or fewer where appropriate.

| Rank / ID | Severity / confidence | Clause / page or omission | Risk and affected party | Singapore authority / pinpoint | Proposed fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

For each finding, provide the relevant clause quotation, realistic trigger scenario, financial/career/business consequence, rating rationale, verified legal proposition and application, credible counterargument or uncertainty, proposed wording or concrete change, fallback and residual risk. Mark whether the correction addresses mandatory compliance or commercial negotiation.

Finish with missing documents/facts, immediate deadlines, unverified concerns and an authority register with access/version dates. If asked for drafting, make wording consistent with incentive plans, notice, deductions, confidentiality and post-employment provisions; do not merely add a general compliance clause over a contradictory term.

## Final quality check

Confirm that every ranked legal finding has a supporting pinpoint and applicability analysis; statutory coverage is not inferred from title alone; no guideline or future reform is misdescribed; monetary examples use disclosed inputs; and proposed remedies do not forfeit other rights inadvertently. Do not give unconditional clearance if missing coverage facts or material plans could change the conclusion.
